OUR STORY

A little warmth.
A lot of care.

In Korean, sodam speaks to something pleasing and abundant. For us, it’s a table filled with good food and the people you want to share it with.

We take our inspiration from the everyday Korean kitchen: the slow heat of charcoal, the comfort of a simmering stew, and small dishes that keep the conversation going.
